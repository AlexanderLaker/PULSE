Value Chain Exposure — position slider concepts

PRISM · Trends page redesign proposal · 2026-07-10 · Maritime tokens from lib/theme.ts. Replaces the per-step 0–5 dot grid with a positional reading of where along the chain (Raw Materials → Consumer) a trend hits. Each concept is shown on all three surfaces: Trend List, Expert Rating, Review & Endorse.

Data-model note. The engine consumes eight per-step vc_exposure scores. Concepts B and C keep that model untouched (the position is a derived, exposure-weighted centre). Concept A makes position + breadth the primary input and expands them into the eight scores (with a per-step Advanced view) — simplest to read, but it changes the input grammar. Example trend: C-r18 · Social commerce & shoppable media; AI profile [0,0,1,1,2,4,5,4] → centre 6.4, Marketing–Commercial.

Concept A — Chain Track (position pin + impact span)

A true slider: navy pin = centre of impact, soft band = breadth. Expert Rating: drag the pin, pick a breadth. Review: AI ◆, individual expert dots (violet), expert ø pin (green) on one rail.

Concept D — Epicentre (single-value slider)

The simplest read: one value only — the stage where the trend’s impact centres (its gravity). The slider snaps to one of the eight stages; no breadth, no profile. Review & Endorse treats stages as categorical: every expert vote is a dot on its stage — votes are counted, never averaged (a Supply Chain vote and two Commercial votes never blend into “Marketing”) — and the admin endorses the majority, the AI stage, or any stage directly.
